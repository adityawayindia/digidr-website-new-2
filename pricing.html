Before you begin

A little more clarity.

Answers to common questions about plans, billing and getting started.

Is Lite free, and can I start with a paid plan?

Lite is free, with no card needed. You can start with Lite or choose Classic, Premium or Pro during onboarding. Lite includes appointment requests; online booking and payments are included with the paid plans.

How are six- and twelve-month plans billed?

The amounts shown per month are equivalents. Six-month plans are paid upfront for six months; annual plans are paid upfront for twelve months. The complete term amount appears on each card. Plan features stay the same when you change the term.

What happens after the introductory offer?

Each paid plan shows a published subsequent monthly rate for the selected term. Confirm the complete renewal amount, the offer period and your renewal schedule with DigiDr before payment.

Will content go live without my approval?

No. Every plan includes doctor approval before publication. You review proposed content before it is released to the agreed professional channels.

Which languages are included?

Lite and Classic include English. Premium adds one regional language. Pro includes English, Hindi and one regional language. Confirm your preferred languages and channels during onboarding.

How do I get started or ask about a plan?

Choose a plan below and register your interest. The DigiDr team will help with your profile, practice details and onboarding. For questions about billing, upgrades, cancellation or refunds, contact info@digidr.app and review the service terms.
